PROJECT RATIONALE

Standard Bank is more than a bank — it's a partner in people's next. 'Enjoy Your Happinext' celebrates the moments that move you forward, no matter how small.

The campaign brings optimism, confidence and colour to the streets — reminding communities across Joburg that their next is worth enjoying.

INSPIRE

CELEBRATE

Champion progress in every form — big or small.

CREATIVE APPROACH

We created a bold, hand-drawn typographic world that feels authentic, youthful and unapologetically South African.

The Happinext typography is playful, imperfect and full of texture — inspired by street art, community signage and everyday creativity.
